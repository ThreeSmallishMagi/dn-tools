Line 35:Rejected: spatial_floydsteinberg dropping residual on the last row is the design, not a bug. Out-of-column taps are excluded from the denominator so nothing leaks sideways; below-row taps stay in it and are not deposited, so the share aimed past the last row falls off the bottom. Renormalising there, as suggested, would concentrate the whole residual into the last row's next pixel. Adding a comment so this is not filed a third time.
